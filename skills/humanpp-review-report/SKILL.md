# humanpp-review-report

Review work produced by a human, agent, or previous implementation session and produce a clear result/report.

Coverage: `Review -> Result`

- blocker
- risk
- suggestion
- nit

## Required outputs

- Review verdict
- Scope compliance
- Blockers
- Risks
- Suggestions
- Validation gaps
- Required follow-up
- Final decision
